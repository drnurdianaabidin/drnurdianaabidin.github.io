[PhysioVerse 3D](/academic/2026-physioverse-3d/), an interactive platform for teaching exercise biomechanics, received a Gold Medal in the Professional category at the Virtual Innovation Competition 2026 (VIC2026).

VIC2026 was an international event organised by DIGIT360, a Universiti Teknologi MARA start-up company, and Universiti Teknologi MARA. The award was made on 25 June 2026 to Dr Nurdiana and to Universiti Sains Malaysia.
