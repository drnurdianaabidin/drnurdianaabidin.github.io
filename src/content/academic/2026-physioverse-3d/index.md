**Role:** Founder and developer

PhysioVerse 3D is an interactive digital learning platform for teaching exercise biomechanics, movement analysis and muscle activation patterns. Animated 3D models demonstrate selected exercises, supported by structured content on primary and secondary muscles, joint actions, movement mechanics, common errors, coaching cues and clinical relevance.

The platform supports visual and self-directed learning among students in Clinical Exercise Science and related health science disciplines, and helps bridge theoretical knowledge with applied movement analysis.

## Key features

- Interactive 3D exercise demonstrations
- Exercise-specific muscle activation mapping
- Biomechanics and joint movement explanations
- Clinical and educational notes for applied exercise physiology
- Web-based access for flexible student learning
