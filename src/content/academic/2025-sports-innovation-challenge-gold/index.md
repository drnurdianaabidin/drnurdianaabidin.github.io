The research on a [resveratrol-loaded dissolving microneedle patch](/research/2022-resveratrol-microneedle-patch/) received a Gold Medal and was First Runner-Up in the Special Category at the USM–AUSC International Sports Innovation Challenge 2025.

The project is ongoing and at the research prototype stage.
