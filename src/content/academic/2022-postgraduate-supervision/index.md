## Research areas

The projects span:

- clinical exercise science and rehabilitation, including exercise therapy after knee surgery
- musculoskeletal health and healthy ageing, including falls prevention among older adults in nursing homes
- exercise psychology, including mental training in sport and tai chi
- athletic performance
- exercise-based interventions, including eccentric exercise for weight management, and exercise combined with dietary habits
- biomedical and digital health research
