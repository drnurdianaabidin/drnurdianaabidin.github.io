**Role:** Founder and developer

A digital quiz application for MSc Clinical Exercise Science students to revise key concepts across the core modules. Structured practice questions encourage frequent revision, familiarity with terminology and self-directed learning outside class hours.

The app was created as a teaching innovation to improve student engagement, reinforce applied clinical knowledge, and let students monitor their own progress.

## Key features

- Questions organised by module
- Multiple-choice practice questions
- Immediate feedback on answers
- Progress tracking visible only to the student
- Works on the web and on mobile phones
- Designed for repeated practice
