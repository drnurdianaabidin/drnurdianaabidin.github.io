## About the review

The review compared artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) models with conventional statistical models, logistic regression and Cox proportional hazards regression, used for prediction in cancer. It assessed their methodological performance, validation strategies and limitations for use in practice.

It included 65 studies: 35 on breast cancer, 21 on colorectal cancer and 9 on pancreatic cancer. Whether AI/ML models outperformed the conventional models depended on the prediction task and the data.
