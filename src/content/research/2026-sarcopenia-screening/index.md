## [[Background]]

[[Sarcopenia is the loss of muscle strength and mass with age. It is linked to falls, frailty and loss of independence. Simple tests such as handgrip strength and walking speed can be carried out in a community setting with little equipment.]]

## [[What the project does]]

[[The project invites adults aged 60 and over attending community clinics to take part in a short assessment. Trained staff measure grip strength, walking speed and body composition. The results are compared with a full clinical assessment.]]

## [[Who is involved]]

[[The project is a collaboration between the Department of Community Health, two community clinics and a group of postgraduate students. Each partner is named in the list on this page.]]
