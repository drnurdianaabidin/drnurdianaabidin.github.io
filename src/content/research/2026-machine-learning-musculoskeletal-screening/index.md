## What the project involves

This research develops and evaluates prediction models for musculoskeletal health outcomes, using large population datasets such as the US National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES).

The work focuses on:

- low appendicular lean mass, a measure of muscle in the arms and legs used when assessing sarcopenia
- low bone mineral density
- developing and validating prediction models
- comparing machine learning methods with conventional screening approaches
- how useful the models would be in practice, and how well they generalise to other populations

A particular interest is populations in which conventional screening methods may be less effective.

## Status

Ongoing research. The models are research tools. They have not been approved or deployed for clinical diagnosis.
